our story

Born from the Boardroom and the Weekend

THE ATELIER was born in Johannesburg — in the space between meetings and midnight. we design leather goods for those who don't ask for permission. structured totes for the professional closing deals. slinky clutches for the night that follows.

every piece is cut from full-grain South African leather, hand-finished with solid brass hardware, and lined with intention. no logos screaming for attention — only quiet, brutal confidence.
